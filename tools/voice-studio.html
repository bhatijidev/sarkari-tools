Processing pipeline

01

Frequency decomposition

Audio tracks are framed into 2048-point windows with 75% overlap. The lowest-energy bins are sampled to construct a baseline noise profile.

02

Spectral gating & EQ

Stationary hum and hiss are subtracted with smoothed gain curves. A 150 Hz low shelf and 3.2 kHz peaking filter shape speech intelligibility.

03

Lossless stream remux

When video files are processed, the video stream is copied bit-for-bit without recompression, keeping original visual resolution and frame rates.
