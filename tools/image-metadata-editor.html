Complete Guide to Image Metadata, EXIF & AI Photo Conversions

1

How AI Metadata Works

Generative AI models like Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and DALL-E automatically save prompt text, negative prompts, seed numbers, and software signatures inside hidden image chunks (PNG text headers, XMP packets, or C2PA metadata). This makes AI images easily detectable by stock platforms and audit software.

2

Converting AI to Camera EXIF

Our tool renders your image onto an in-browser canvas to completely destroy every hidden AI prompt and tracking marker. Then, it injects genuine EXIF IFD data from authentic DSLR and smartphone cameras (Sony A7 IV, Canon EOS R5, iPhone 15 Pro) with accurate focal lengths, f-stops, and shutter speeds.

3

100% Privacy & GPS Stripping

Every photo taken with a smartphone contains precise GPS latitude and longitude coordinates showing where you live, work, or travel. The 'Strip All Metadata' option strips 100% of geotags and device serial numbers before sharing online or submitting to exam forms.
