How to Write Bank Cheques & Legal Receipts Correctly

1

Always Append "Only"

Writing "Only" at the end of the amount string (e.g. Fifty Thousand Rupees Only) prevents unauthorized alteration or tampering on paper cheques.

2

Indian vs International Standards

Indian banking adheres to the Lakh (100,000) and Crore (10,000,000) convention, rather than Millions and Billions.

3

Decimal Paise Representation

Amounts containing decimals (e.g., .50) are formally written as "and Fifty Paise Only" to ensure exact financial reconciliation.
