Exam Qualification Norms:

  • SSC CHSL English Typing: Minimum 35 WPM (10500 KDPH) with up to 5% allowed mistakes for General, 7% for OBC/SC/ST.
  • SSC CHSL Hindi Typing: Minimum 30 WPM (9000 KDPH).
  • Court Typist / Steno: Minimum 35 - 40 WPM with 95%+ accuracy.
