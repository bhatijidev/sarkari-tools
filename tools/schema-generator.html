How Does Schema Markup Boost Google Rankings?

Schema markup is a standard code vocabulary agreed upon by Google, Microsoft, and Yahoo. When you implement JSON-LD Schema markup on your web pages, Google Search can display eye-catching Rich Snippets—such as FAQ dropdown accordions, gold review stars (★ 4.8), product pricing, and verified author badges. These rich elements drastically increase your organic Click-Through Rate (CTR) by up to 35%.

Recommended Google Schema Types:

  • FAQPage: Best for guides, tool landing pages, and exam portals to dominate SERP real estate.
  • LocalBusiness: Crucial for cyber cafes, photostats, coaching institutes, and local shops in India to rank in Google Maps 3-Pack.
  • Article / NewsArticle: Essential for Google Discover and Google News eligibility.
