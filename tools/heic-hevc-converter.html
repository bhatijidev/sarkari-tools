Why Don't iPhone Photos & Videos Open on Android Phones?

By default, Apple devices (iPhone 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, and 16) capture photos in HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) and record videos in HEVC / H.265 (High Efficiency Video Coding) inside .MOV containers. While these formats save storage on Apple hardware, most Android devices, older Windows PCs, and third-party apps like WhatsApp lack hardware licensing for HEVC or cannot parse HEIC image compression.
