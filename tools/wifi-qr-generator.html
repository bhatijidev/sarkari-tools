Why Use Wi-Fi QR Codes for Cafes, Hotels & Offices?

1

Eliminate Password Typing Errors

Complex Wi-Fi passwords with special characters and uppercase letters cause frustration and repeated customer complaints. A QR code connects everyone in under 1 second.

2

Enhanced Network Security

Guests never see or copy your raw password characters. You can set a strong 20-character password without worrying that customers won't be able to type it.

3

Table Tent & Menu Integration

Print the QR standee and place it on restaurant tables, reception desks, or co-working space walls so visitors connect immediately on arrival.

4

Native iOS & Android Support

Both Apple iPhones (iOS 11+) and Android smartphones (Android 9+) have native Wi-Fi QR detection built directly into their camera apps.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the syntax of a Wi-Fi QR code?

It uses the international standard format: WIFI:T:WPA;S:MyNetworkName;P:MyPassword;;. When a phone camera detects this prefix, it routes it directly to the system Wi-Fi manager.

Q: What if I change my Wi-Fi password in the future?

Because static Wi-Fi QR codes contain the password inside the image, if you change your router password, simply generate and print a fresh QR code here for free.
