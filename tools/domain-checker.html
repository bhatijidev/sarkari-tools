How Does Our Live Domain Availability Checker Work?

Unlike basic tools that rely on outdated cached databases, our tool performs live dynamic queries using DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) directly to global authoritative root servers (Cloudflare and Google). If the root servers return NXDOMAIN (Non-Existent Domain), the domain name is currently unregistered and available to buy. If authoritative SOA or NS records exist, the domain is already registered.

Which Domain Extension (TLD) Should You Choose in India?

  • .IN (India): Highly recommended for cyber cafes, sarkari exam guides, coaching institutes, and businesses targeting Indian students and users. Managed by NIXI (National Internet Exchange of India).
  • .COM (Commercial): The global gold standard. Best for international credibility and universal trust.
  • .CO.IN: Traditional Indian business extension, affordable and trusted by millions of local companies.
  • .ORG: Best for educational trusts, non-profits, student communities, and NGOs.
