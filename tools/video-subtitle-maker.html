How to Make Subtitles from a Video — Step by Step

  1. Load media: drop your video (or just an audio/podcast file). It plays on the right — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  2. Run the AI: pick language (Hindi / English / auto), choose Tiny / Base / Small model and press "Auto-Generate Subtitles". The Whisper model runs inside your browser.
  3. Edit on the timeline: each subtitle is a block — drag to move, pull edges to adjust start/end. In the cue list you can retype text, split or merge cues, and nudge timings by ±0.1s.
  4. Hinglish mode: toggle "Hinglish (Roman)" to show Devanagari subtitles in Roman script — great for reels and shorts.
  5. Export: download the finished SRT (YouTube / VLC / editors) or VTT (web players), or a plain text transcript.
