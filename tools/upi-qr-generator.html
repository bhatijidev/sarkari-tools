Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Where can I find my UPI ID / VPA?

Open any UPI app on your phone (Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm). Tap on your profile photo at the top. Your UPI ID will be displayed (for example: yourname@okhdfcbank, 9876543210@ybl, or shopname@paytm).

Q: Is there any limit on payments received via this QR?

Standard peer-to-peer (P2P) and peer-to-merchant (P2M) UPI limits apply as set by your bank and NPCI (typically up to ₹1,00,000 to ₹2,00,000 per day for verified merchant accounts).

Q: Are my banking details stored on your server?

No. AllToolsInOne runs 100% in your local browser using client-side JavaScript and Canvas API. Your UPI ID, payee name, and payment data never touch any server.
