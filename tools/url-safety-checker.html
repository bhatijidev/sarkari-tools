How to Identify Dangerous & Scam Links Before Clicking

Cybercriminals frequently send deceptive SMS and WhatsApp messages claiming your electricity will be disconnected, bank KYC is expired, or you won a prize. Here is how you can protect yourself:

Top 5 Red Flags of Scam & Phishing Links:

  • Newly Registered Domains (NRD): Over 85% of phishing portals are hosted on domains registered less than 15 days ago. Always check domain age before trusting a site.
  • Fake Bank KYC & Bill Payment Triggers: Messages like "Dear Customer, your SBI/HDFC account will be blocked today, update KYC here" are 100% fraud. Banks never send links to update personal information or download APK files.
  • URL Shorteners (Bit.ly, TinyURL): Scammers hide the true malicious destination behind link shorteners so you cannot see the real domain name.
  • Homograph & Lookalike Typos: Attackers replace English characters with similar-looking characters (like Cyrillic 'а' in `pаypal.com`) or insert hyphens (like `sbi-netbanking-portal.com`).
  • Insecure HTTP Connections: If a link starts with `http://` instead of `https://`, any login password or card number you enter can be intercepted by hackers on the network.
