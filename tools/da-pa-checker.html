What are Domain Authority (DA) and Page Authority (PA)?

Domain Authority (DA) is a search engine ranking score developed to predict how likely a website is to rank on search engine result pages (SERPs). Scores range from 1 to 100, with higher scores corresponding to a greater ability to rank. Page Authority (PA) measures the predictive ranking strength of a single individual page rather than the whole domain.

What is a Good DA Score?

  • DA 1 to 20: Brand new websites or small personal blogs just getting started.
  • DA 21 to 40: Growing regional websites, established local cyber cafes, niche portals.
  • DA 41 to 60: Strong authoritative sites with solid backlink profiles and high organic traffic.
  • DA 61 to 100: Giant national and global authority sites (e.g. Sarkari Result, Wikipedia, UPSC, Indian Government portals).
