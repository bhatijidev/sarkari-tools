What is the Ideal Keyword Density for SEO?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a keyword or phrase appears on a web page compared to the total number of words. The widely accepted industry standard for optimal SEO keyword density is between 1% to 2.5%. Exceeding 3% can trigger Google's spam filters for keyword stuffing, whereas under 0.5% may fail to signal search engines what your page is about.

How to Optimize Content Naturally:

  • Use LSI (Latent Semantic Indexing) keywords and synonyms rather than repeating the exact same word 20 times.
  • Focus on 2-word and 3-word long-tail keywords (e.g. "how to prepare ssc cgl exam at home") which carry high conversion intent.
  • Keep your Flesch Reading Ease score above 60 (Plain English) for general audience blogs and exam portals.
