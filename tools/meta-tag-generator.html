Why are SEO Meta Tags & Open Graph Critical?

Meta tags communicate vital details to search engines like Google and Bing. An optimized Title Tag directly influences search rankings and click-through rates (CTR). Open Graph (og:) tags ensure that when someone shares your page on WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, or Telegram, an attractive image card with clean title and description appears instead of a bare, clickable link.

Best Practices for Maximum Search Clicks:

  • Place your primary targeted keyword near the beginning of your Title tag.
  • Write an action-oriented Meta Description (include words like Free, Apply Online, Download, Check).
  • Use 1200×630 px images for Open Graph to prevent blurry thumbnails on WhatsApp & Facebook.
  • Always set a canonical tag to avoid duplicate content penalties from URL parameters.
