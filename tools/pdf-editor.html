How to Edit a PDF — Step by Step

  1. Load PDFs: drop one or many files above. Multiple files are merged into one document.
  2. Organize pages: in the Pages grid, rotate (↺ ↻), delete (✕), duplicate (⧉) or move (◀ ▶) pages to reorder.
  3. Annotate: open a page, then Add Text, Add Image or Draw Signature. Drag to position, corner handle to resize, black toolbar to restyle.
  4. Stamp: switch on Watermark for all pages, or add automatic page numbers.
  5. Export: press the green button — the edited PDF is generated in your browser and downloaded instantly.
