How to Tip in the US and Canada

In North America, restaurant waitstaff often earn a "tipped minimum wage" that is significantly lower than standard state minimum wage. Tips make up the majority of their income. Here is the general tipping standard:

  • 15%: Average / fair service.
  • 18%: Good standard service at casual and sit-down restaurants.
  • 20%: Great service, attentive staff, fine dining.
  • 22% - 25%: Exceptional experience, large party service.
  • Bartenders: $1 to $2 per drink or 20% of the bar tab.
  • Food Delivery Drivers: $3 to $5 minimum, or 15% - 20% on larger orders.
