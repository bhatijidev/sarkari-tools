How Does the Online Net Speed Test Work?

This speed test tool directly connects to distributed high-speed content delivery networks (CDNs) to benchmark your real-time internet throughput without third-party bloatware.

⬇️ Download Speed

Measures how fast content (web pages, YouTube videos, files) downloads to your computer or phone. Expressed in Megabits per second (Mbps).

⬆️ Upload Speed

Crucial for video calls, uploading YouTube videos, cloud backups, and sending emails with large attachments.

⏱️ Ping & Jitter

Ping measures responsiveness (latency) in milliseconds (ms). Low ping (< 30ms) is essential for online multiplayer games like BGMI, Free Fire, and Valorant.
