Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026 • Effective immediately for all visitors

Summary: At AllToolsInOne (accessible at alltoolsinone.com), your privacy is our top priority. Our 73+ tools run 100% locally in your web browser. We do NOT upload, inspect, or store your documents, PDFs, photos, or data on our servers.

1. Client-Side Processing Architecture (Zero Server Uploads)

Unlike conventional web applications that upload your confidential files to remote cloud servers for processing, AllToolsInOne is engineered on modern client-side technologies (HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, Web Audio API, and local JavaScript).

  • Image & Photo Tools: Cropping, resizing, background removal, and compression occur strictly in your device's memory.
  • PDF & Document Tools: Merging, splitting, watermarking, locking, and viewing PDFs are handled locally by your browser.
  • Calculators & Code Converters: All calculations, Base64 encodings, and hash operations execute locally on your machine without transmitting input to external backends.

Once you close or reload the browser tab, any temporary data held in local browser memory is instantly cleared.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use third-party advertising companies to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Our primary advertising partner is Google AdSense.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on AllToolsInOne.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites across the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you may opt out of third-party vendor cookie usage for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at networkadvertising.org or Digital Advertising Alliance at aboutads.info.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks employ technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their advertisements and links that appear on AllToolsInOne. These are sent directly to your browser and automatically receive your IP address. These technologies measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and personalize ad content.

Note that AllToolsInOne has no access to or control over cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any modern website, AllToolsInOne uses 'cookies'. Cookies are small files stored on your device that retain visitor preferences (such as Dark Mode/Light Mode settings) and record anonymous session identifiers.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Comprehensive information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective official websites.

4. Web Analytics and Log Files

AllToolsInOne follows standard procedures for utilizing log files. These files log visitors when they access websites. Information gathered includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks.

These records are not linked to any personally identifiable information. The purpose is strictly analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking aggregate movements across pages, and diagnosing technical issues.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights (European Users)

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all your data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request restriction of personal data processing.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request transfer of collected data to another organization.

Because we do not require account registration and process files client-side, we do not maintain identifiable user profiles on our servers.

6. CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • The right to know the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • The right to request deletion of personal data collected.
  • The right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal data.

AllToolsInOne does not sell personal information to any third parties.

7. Children's Information (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young children using the internet is especially important. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity.

AllToolsInOne does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such records from our systems.
